1. What Are Cookies

1.1 Cookies, also known as browser cookies or tracking cookies, are small, often encrypted text files located in browser directories. They are used by web developers to help users navigate websites efficiently and to perform certain functions. The term also encompasses a range of technologies, including but not limited to:

  • (a) pixel tags, which indicate whether a page or email has been viewed;
  • (b) mobile device identifiers; and
  • (c) web storage used on both desktop and mobile devices.

1.2 Cookies can generally be broken down into two categories:

  • (a) Session cookies, which expire once you close the website; and
  • (b) Persistent cookies, which stay attached to your browser until you delete them.

1.3 There is also a distinction between cookies set by the website you visit, known as first-party cookies, and third-party cookies. Third-party cookies are usually set by the partners of the site you visit; Google Analytics is a good example.

1.4 We use a combination of cookies throughout our platforms to improve our services, as detailed in this Policy. Cookies also help us remember your preferences.

The following table shows the different cookies we use in order to provide you with the best possible service. If you have any questions regarding the cookies we use, please refer to the contact section of this Policy.

Account-related cookiesIf you create an account with us, we will use cookies for the management of the signup process and for general administration. 3. Customising Your Cookie Preferences

We want you to have as much control over your cookie traceability as possible. If you want to check or change what types of cookies you accept, this can be done within your browser settings or directly from our site. Please note that some third-party partners and affiliates, including advertising networks and providers of external services such as web traffic analysis, may have cookie policies of their own over which we have no control. Should you wish to change your third-party cookie preferences, you can do so in your browser settings or on the sites of those third-party providers.
