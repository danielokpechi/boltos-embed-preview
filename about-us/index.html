About Us

The world of content is at a critical juncture and BoltOS is helping to shape its future.

For sports rights holders and media owners, BoltOS is the audience revenue engine that transforms viewer interaction into measurable sponsorship and advertising revenue. Unlike engagement tools or video platforms, BoltOS integrates seamlessly into existing ecosystems, enabling IP owners to activate, monetize, retain and own their audience without disrupting their current stack.

Our Timeline

Where We've Been and Where We're Going

2017

Founded the company.

2018

Bolt+ emerged as a next-generation streaming platform designed to elevate audience engagement and create truly immersive viewing experiences. Initially developed as standalone mobile and web applications, Bolt+ successfully integrated directly into telecommunication networks across Asia-Pacific, establishing a proven foundation for scalable, operator-grade streaming solutions.

2019

We debuted our large-scale live sports capabilities through our successful carriage of 2019 Rugby World Cup and Cricket World Cup content, establishing our expertise in delivering premium sporting content to global audiences.

2021

We revolutionized the smart TV landscape by launching BoltOS on Hisense and VIDAA devices, catalysing an ecosystem expansion that attracted global media powerhouses including Al Jazeera, Deutsche Welle, Euronews, and One Football.

2022

Our reimagined Bolt+ streaming platform delivers breakthrough immersive streaming experiences, creating a unified destination where emerging creators and media giants converge, fundamentally redefining how audiences connect with content.

2023

With the release of Immersive Platform 1.0, we set the foundation for next-generation media tools that transform content engagement. This innovation propelled strategic partnerships with Bloomberg and OTTera, who recognised BoltOS as the definitive platform for content delivery and monetisation.

2024

Our strategic evolution to SaaS delivery has unlocked exponential growth opportunities, while securing transformative partnerships with industry leaders including VIZIO, TheSoul Publishing, Netrange, and TitanOS, positioning BoltOS as the platform powering the future of media. We strengthened our track record in high-stakes sporting event delivery through successful deployments with the Hankook 24 Hours Dubai and the 2024 Women's Baseball and Softball World Cups.
