Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical expertise to use BoltOS products?

Not at all. Our products are designed for media teams, not developers. The control panel designed to manage them is also intuitive, and we provide guidance and workshops to educate your teams on best practices.

How long does setup take?

Setup once the brief is finalized can take anywhere from two to four weeks, depending on our workload at the time. However, we always endeavor to deliver in the shortest time frame possible while not compromising on quality.

What if my broadcasting platform isn't listed in your integrations?

We add new integrations regularly based on customer demand. If your platform isn't currently supported, contact our team. We can often create custom integrations or use other means of connection.

Can viewers access interactive features on mobile devices?

Yes. All our interactive features are fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop. Your audience can participate regardless of device.

How many concurrent viewers can your system handle?

Our infrastructure auto-scales to handle unlimited concurrent users. We've successfully supported broadcasts with 250,000+ simultaneous viewers without performance degradation.

Can I customize the look and feel to match my brand?

Absolutely. You have complete control over colors, fonts, layouts, and branding. We offer templates for quick setup, or you can create fully custom designs. Enterprise plans include white-label options with custom domains.

How does monetization work?

Panels contain banner ads that will generate ad dollars. Viewers can also make purchases or unlock premium content directly within your panel. We will work with you to ensure your preferred major payment gateways are added.

What happens if something breaks during a live broadcast?

We provide 24/7 support for Critical and High priority issues, including weekends and holidays. If you experience a critical failure during a live broadcast, our team responds within 5 minutes and aims for resolution within 1 hour. We take your reputation as seriously as you do.

What's your uptime guarantee?

We maintain 99.9% uptime backed by redundant infrastructure and continuous monitoring. Our status page provides real-time system health updates.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

We're GDPR compliant and use end-to-end encryption. Your data and your audience's data are protected with enterprise-grade security.
