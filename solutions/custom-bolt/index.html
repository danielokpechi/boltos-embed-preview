Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical expertise to use Custom Bolt+?

Not at all. Custom Bolt+ is designed to make your life easier. You get access to an intuitive and user-friendly control panel, and we provide guidance every step of the way during implementation and recommendation on the best practices as you start your journey with us.

What if my broadcasting platform isn't listed in your integrations?

We add new integrations regularly. If your platform isn't currently supported, contact our team, we can create custom integrations or use other means of connection.

Can audiences access Custom Bolt+ on mobile devices?

Yes! Custom Bolt+ is fully optimised for mobile, tablet, and desktop. Your audience can connect and participate regardless of device.

How does BoltOS handle large live audiences?

Custom Bolt+ automatically scales to support unlimited concurrent users during live broadcasts. We have successfully supported events with more than 250,000 simultaneous viewers without any loss of performance, while continuous monitoring helps maintain stability as audience demand changes.

Can I customise the look and feel of Custom Bolt+ to match my brand?

Absolutely. You have full control over the colours, fonts, layouts, and branding. We offer templates for quick setup, or you can create fully bespoke designs.

What happens if something breaks during a live broadcast?

We provide 24/7 support for critical and high priority issues, including weekends and public holidays. If you experience a critical failure during a live broadcast, our team responds within 5 minutes and aims to resolve the issue within one hour. We take your reputation as seriously as you do.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

We're GDPR compliant and use end-to-end encryption. Your data and your audience's data are protected with enterprise-grade security.
