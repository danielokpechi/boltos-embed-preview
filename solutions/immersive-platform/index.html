What is the Immersive Platform?

The Immersive Platform is a drop-in interactive overlay that transforms any live or on-demand stream or event into an interactive, monetisable experience.

It works in two ways:

01

Engagement shifts to social platforms you don't control

02

Direct integration into your web or app via code embed

Either way, your viewing experience stays intact. The monetisation and intelligence layer activates around it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need technical expertise to use the Immersive Platform?

Not at all. Our solutions are designed for media and entertainment teams with busy schedules. You get access to an intuitive and user-friendly control panel, and we provide guidance every step of the way during implementation and recommendation on the best practices as you start your journey with us.

How long does setup take?

Setup typically takes between two to four weeks. Let us know in advance if you have a key date to launch so we can adapt the timeline.

What are the types of integrations you offer?

We regularly add new integrations. If a platform is not currently supported, contact our team, we can create custom integrations or connect through alternative methods.

Where can audiences access the Immersive platform?

The immersive platform can be accessed from anywhere in the world and is fully optimised for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Can I customise the look and feel of the Immersive Platform to match my brand?

Absolutely. You have full control over the colours, fonts, layouts, and branding. We offer templates for quick setup, or you can create fully bespoke designs.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

We're GDPR compliant and use end-to-end encryption. Your data and your audience's data are protected with enterprise-grade security.
